About Me

I am a computer science researcher and software engineer with roughly ten years of experience across both. My broader interests include machine learning, software architecture, and formal methods. I currently work as a quantitative developer building trading strategies and tools at SIG.

I completed my PhD at Drexel University, advised by Dr. Yuanfang Cai and co-advised by Dr. Rick Kazman. My research focused on custom LLM-derived embeddings that automatically recover the responsibilities and design concerns needed to guide software refactoring.

During my PhD, I did research internships at AWS with Dr. Martin Schäf and at NEC Labs with Dr. Tingfeng Li. Earlier, I was a software engineer at WebstaurantStore under Bill Blaettler, and did undergraduate research at YCP with Dr. David Hovemeyer.
